Bar Council of India Rule 36 Statutory Immunity

Under Section IV, Rule 36 of Bar Council of India Rules, legal counsel are prohibited from advertising. However, providing essential directory information (contact coordinates, courts of enrollment, credentials, and practice areas) upon specific visitor request is 100% lawful. Every legal design in our catalogue includes the statutory compliance disclaimer by default.
