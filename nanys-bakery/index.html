01 / The Oven Ritual

Good baking cannot be hurried. Time is our most essential ingredient.

At Nany's Bakery, every loaf of sourdough is shaped by hand and naturally fermented for over 36 hours. Our laminations use 100% pure unsalted dairy butter to achieve that signature honeycomb interior and fragile, shattering crust.

Whether you stop by for a morning espresso and fresh croissant or order a bespoke tiered cake for a mountain wedding, you taste the dedication of artisanal European baking right in Dehradun.
